*Software Engineering at Google* ("the Flamingo book", O'Reilly 2020) is Titus Winters, Tom Manshreck & Hyrum Wright's field guide to engineering at scale. Its thesis fits in one line: **software engineering is programming integrated over time** — the passage of time, the scale of the organization, and the trade-offs made along the way are what separate engineering from programming.

The book's three lenses map to its parts:

1. **Culture** — teams, knowledge sharing, leadership, and how to measure productivity without breaking it.
2. **Process** — why rules exist, code review, documentation, and a deep testing philosophy (Google's test pyramid, test doubles, and deprecation as a process).
3. **Tools** — version control, code search, build systems, static analysis, CI/CD, and large-scale changes.
